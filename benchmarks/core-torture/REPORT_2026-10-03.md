## Important multiplayer finding

The cross-client probe demonstrates current behavior:

```text
Client A ─┐
          ├── same Session → both service calls may be in flight
Client B ─┘
```

There is no single-writer arbitration in `SessionRouter`.

This is not necessarily a runtime defect.

It means policy such as:

- one steering owner;
- human-preempts-agent;
- explicit writer lease;
- priority steering;

belongs above the transport/session router unless a universal core invariant is identified.
